# Choosing ice cream at a shop

1. Ask only the useful quick questions:
   - Do they want something familiar, adventurous, or a balance?
   - Which flavor direction sounds best: chocolate, vanilla/caramel, fruity, nutty, coffee, mint, or something else?
   - Do they prefer creamy and rich, light and refreshing, or lots of mix-ins?
   - Any allergies, dietary restrictions, or ingredients to avoid?
   - Cone or cup, and how many scoops?
2. If they are already looking at a menu, ask them to share the available flavors. Do not recommend unavailable flavors.
3. Recommend one clear first choice and up to two backups. For each, give a short reason tied to their preferences.
4. If they are torn between two flavors, use a simple tie-breaker:
   - Choose the familiar flavor for reliable comfort.
   - Choose the unusual flavor for novelty.
   - Choose one scoop of each if the shop allows mixing.
5. Mention cross-contamination or uncertain ingredients when allergies are involved, and suggest confirming with shop staff. Never treat a flavor name as proof that it is allergy-safe.
6. Keep the response concise and practical, as if helping someone decide while standing at the counter.
